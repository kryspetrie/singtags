# Chord charts by key — spellings you can sing

**Goal:** Look up **exact note names** for contest chords in the keys barbershop uses most — not only C.

**How to use:** Pick your song key → find I / IV / V / V7 / II7 → then jump to “all 11 on this root” when you need color chords.

**Spelling rule:** We use the accidentals of the **key signature** when possible; raised tones in secondary dominants use sharps (F♯ in D7 even in flat keys). Dim7 spelling prefers readable flats or the conventional enharmonic for that root.

---

## 1. How every contest chord is built

**Canonical formulas / when-to-use:** [12-eleven-chords-ji.md](12-eleven-chords-ji.md). This chapter is the **spelling lookup** — same intervals, many keys.

From a **root**, stack these intervals (semitones in parentheses):

| Chord (app id) | Formula from root | Roles | Plain English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Major** (`major`) | 1 – M3 – P5 (0–4–7) | 1 3 5 | Happy triad |
| **Minor** (`minor`) | 1 – m3 – P5 (0–3–7) | 1 ♭3 5 | Sad triad |
| **Aug** (`aug`) | 1 – M3 – A5 (0–4–8) | 1 3 ♯5 | Raised 5th |
| **Dim triad** (`dim`) | 1 – m3 – d5 (0–3–6) | 1 ♭3 ♭5 | BHS-extended |
| **BS7 / Dom7** (`seventh`) | Major + m7 (0–4–7–10) | 1 3 5 ♭7 | **Barbershop fuel** |
| **Minor 7** (`m7`) | Minor + m7 (0–3–7–10) | 1 ♭3 5 ♭7 | Softer 7th |
| **Half-dim** (`half-dim`) | Dim + m7 (0–3–6–10) | 1 ♭3 ♭5 ♭7 | BHS-extended |
| **Dim7** (`dim7`) | Stack of m3s (0–3–6–9) | 1 ♭3 ♭5 𝄫7 | Transition only |
| **Maj7** (`maj7`) | Major + M7 (0–4–7–11) | 1 3 5 7 | Melody-forced |
| **Add9** (`add9`) | Major + M9 (0–4–7–2) | 1 3 5 9 | No 7th |
| **Sixth** (`sixth`) | Major + M6 (0–4–7–9) | 1 3 5 6 | Often omit 5 |
| **Min+6** (`madd6`) | Minor + M6 (0–3–7–9) | 1 ♭3 5 6 | Rings hard |
| **Dom9** (`ninth`) | BS7 + M9 (0–4–7–10–2) | 1 3 5 ♭7 9 | **Omit one** in 4 parts |

### Build any BS7 in three steps

1. Name the **root** (e.g. E).  
2. Add major triad: E–G♯–B.  
3. Add minor 7th above root: **D** → **E7** = E G♯ B D.

### Roles under the lead

If the lead sings a pitch, it must be one of **1, 3, 5, 6, 7, or 9** of the chord you choose. Example: lead on G♯ can be **3 of E7**, **♯5 of Caug**, etc. — pick the chord that matches your pillar story.

## 3. Four useful minor keys

Barbershop minor still cadences with **major V7** (raised leading tone).

### A minor (relative of C)

| RN | Symbol | Spelling |
| --- | --- |
| i | Am | A C E |
| iv | Dm | D F A |
| V | E | E G♯ B |
| V7 | E7 | E G♯ B D |
| III | C | C E G |
| VI | F | F A C |
| VII | G | G B D |
| II7 (toward V) | B7 | B D♯ F♯ A |
| iiø | Bø7 | B D F A |

Highway (pure Co5 after springboard): `Am → F♯7 → B7 → E7 → Am`  
Color path with R3 (not pure fifths): `Am → C7 → F7 ↔ B7 → E7 → Am` — **F7↔B7 is a tritone counterpart**, not a P5.

### D minor (relative of F)

| RN | Symbol | Spelling |
| --- | --- |
| i | Dm | D F A |
| iv | Gm | G B♭ D |
| V7 | A7 | A C♯ E G |
| III | F | F A C |
| VI | B♭ | B♭ D F |
| II7 | E7 | E G♯ B D |

Highway (pure Co5): `Dm → B7 → E7 → A7 → Dm`  
Color with R3: `Dm → F7 → B♭7 ↔ E7 → A7 → Dm` — **B♭7↔E7 is a tritone**, not a P5.

### E minor (relative of G)

| RN | Symbol | Spelling |
| --- | --- |
| i | Em | E G B |
| iv | Am | A C E |
| V7 | B7 | B D♯ F♯ A |
| III | G | G B D |
| VI | C | C E G |
| II7 | F♯7 | F♯ A♯ C♯ E |

Highway (pure Co5): `Em → F♯7 → B7 → Em` (or deeper `Em → C♯7 → F♯7 → B7 → Em`)

### G minor (relative of B♭)

| RN | Symbol | Spelling |
| --- | --- |
| i | Gm | G B♭ D |
| iv | Cm | C E♭ G |
| V7 | D7 | D F♯ A C |
| III | B♭ | B♭ D F |
| VI | E♭ | E♭ G B♭ |
| II7 | A7 | A C♯ E G |

Highway (pure Co5): `Gm → E7 → A7 → D7 → Gm`

## 5. Dom9 in four parts (omit charts)

Keep **3, ♭7, 9**. Omit root *or* 5th.

| Full Dom9 | Omit root (bass on 5) | Omit 5th (bass on 1) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| G9 = G B D F A | **B D F A** (bass D) | **G B F A** (bass G) |
| C9 = C E G B♭ D | **E G B♭ D** (bass G) | **C E B♭ D** (bass C) |
| F9 = F A C E♭ G | **A C E♭ G** (bass C) | **F A E♭ G** (bass F) |
| B♭9 = B♭ D F A♭ C | **D F A♭ C** (bass F) | **B♭ D A♭ C** (bass B♭) |
| D9 = D F♯ A C E | **F♯ A C E** (bass A) | **D F♯ C E** (bass D) |
| A7’s 9 = A9 = A C♯ E G B | **C♯ E G B** (bass E) | **A C♯ G B** (bass A) |

## 8. Transpose recipe (any key)

1. Write the progression in **Roman numerals** (I–VI7–II7–V7–I).  
2. Map each RN to a root in your key (section 2).  
3. Spell the quality with section 1 formulas / section 4 tables.  
4. Check lead membership: lead PC ∈ chord roles.
