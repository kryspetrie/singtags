# Song selection and form — before you arrange

Source: BAM pp. 27–66; Prietto contest/song guidance.  
**Goal:** Decide whether a melody is *worth* arranging as barbershop — and how its form constrains embellishment.

---

## Why this comes first

Approach Two assumes a **barbershoppable** tune. A Broadway ballad that needs piano, or a melody full of accented maj7 leaps, will fight every later rule.

---

## Melody gates (M-rules)

| Prefer | Avoid / warn |
| --- | --- |
| Fits amateur **lead** range (TTBB ≈ D3–F4 center; SSAA often higher) | Extreme highs/lows as the default tessitura |
| Mostly **diatonic**, stepwise | Disjunct leaps on stress beats |
| More ascending than descending energy | Long descending lines (pitch sag) |
| **Sol→Sol** friendly keys (often A♭–C for men’s) | Awkward **Do→Do** lows in sharp keys |
| Few low 1 / 3 / ♭7 on long stress notes | Melody that *is* the harmony’s awkward tone all phrase |

### Pass / fail sketches

**Friendlier (C):** mostly C–D–E–G–A with cadence to C or E.  
**Harder:** melody hangs on B♮ (maj7 of C) for whole bars on downbeats — forces constant CM7.

---

## Lyric (human judgment)

- Prefer sincere, singable stories; avoid taste problems for contest.  
- Align **lyric climax** with melodic/harmonic climax when possible.  
- SAI contest: Song Assessment may be required — human gate, not code.

---

## Harmony eligibility

Ask: does the melody **imply** I–IV–V / circle motion with room for BS7s?

| Likely yes | Likely no |
| --- | --- |
| Clear cadences; room for G7→C | One chord for 16 bars |
| Occasional chromatic color tones | Requires jazz extensions as the *identity* |
| Minor with clear V→i | Formless verse without pillars |

---

## Form gates (F-rules)

| Prefer | Avoid |
| --- | --- |
| Phrases of **4 or 8** bars | 3 / 5 / 7-bar norms as default |
| Chorus schemes **AABA, ABAB, ABAC** | Blank AAAA or formless pop when contesting |
| Embellish by **filling**, not chopping | Cutting measures to “make room” for a swipe |
| Climax near the end (often with key lift) | Climax in bar 2 then nowhere to go |

### Formal balance

If the song’s chorus is 32 bars ending on I in bar 31–32, a tag should **respect** that cadence placement (even lengths: 2, 4, 8…). Don’t steal a beat from the last bar to shoehorn a swipe.

---

## Worked eligibility pass (toy tune in C)

```
||: C  D  E  G | A  G  E  C | F  A  G  E | D  G  C  C :||
```

- Range: C4–A4 — fine for many leads.  
- Pillars: I … IV … V–I implied.  
- Form: 8+8 repeatable.  
→ **Eligible** to enter Approach Two.

---

## Product step

Run **song eligibility** (range, phrase length, form tag, climax marker) *before* auto-harmonize.
