## Method notes

- **Just intonation only:** pitch-shift targets = `justFundamentals` (role ratios × root Hz, octave-matched to TTBB placement).
- JI intervals verified pairwise (octave-reduced) before scoring.
- Mid/side constant-length pitch shift for dry stacking.
- FFT score on Mid against the JI partial grid of that stack.
- App column = same JI fundamentals through `scoreHarmonicity` port.
- Lead skeleton @ MIDI 67; every catalog voicing scored.
